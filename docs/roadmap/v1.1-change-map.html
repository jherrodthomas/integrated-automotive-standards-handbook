Chapter 8, before and after

Application coding guide becomes a platform software chapter

v1.0 never mentions hypervisors, freedom from interference, SEooC, QNX, Android, Linux or open source. v1.1 adds the layer where ISO 26262 and ISO/SAE 21434 meet on a vehicle computer.

STPA in the concept phase

One control-structure analysis feeds four standards

STPA moves from v2.0 into v1.1. Shared losses and hazards keep its results traceable to the HARA safety goals. The security extension (J3187) stays in v2.0.
